**Short answer:** Yes, quality directly affects sell price. Standard quality products sell at 1.4x suggested price. Premium quality sells at 1.5x. Heavenly quality sells at 1.6x. Quality is determined by growing conditions: fertilizer use, PGR/Speed Grow application, and drying rack processing. A Premium OG Kush 4-mix ($170 base) sells for ~$255 at 1.5x, versus ~$238 for Standard -- a $17 difference per unit.
{{< /callout >}}

This is a data comparison guide -- not a "what is quality" explainer. Use the tables below to decide whether quality upgrades are worth the extra time and cost. For the base profit figures without quality adjustments, see the [recipe profit rankings](/schedule-i/recipes/profit/).

## Quality Tier Reference

Cross-referenced from Fandom Wiki Quality page, TheGamer quality guide, Steam Community pricing guide, and r/Schedule_I community testing.

| Quality Tier | Icon | Sell Price Multiplier | How To Achieve | Extra Requirements |
|-------------|------|---------------------|----------------|-------------------|
| Standard | None (default) | 1.4x | Use PGR or Speed Grow | None (baseline) |
| Premium | Purple star | 1.5x | Use Fertilizer only (no PGR/Speed Grow) | Requires drying rack |
| Heavenly | Gold star | 1.6x | Fertilizer + Drying Rack processing | Requires drying rack + Botanist |

**Source:** TheGamer "Best Quality Types" guide confirms Premium requires Fertilizer only (no Speed Grow or PGR). Fandom Wiki Quality page confirms Heavenly requires drying rack processing. Scaling quality production requires automation — the [Barn vs Warehouse ROI comparison](/schedule-i/automation/barn-and-warehouse/) shows at what setup quality upgrades become profitable.

## Profit Comparison By Quality

Base assumption: OG Kush 4-mix ($170 suggested price, 4 units per harvest).

| Quality | Sell Price (per unit) | Gross Per Harvest | Cost to Achieve | Net Per Harvest | Extra Profit vs Standard |
|---------|---------------------|-------------------|-----------------|----------------|------------------------|
| Standard | $238 (1.4x) | $952 | $0 (baseline) | ~$900 | $0 |
| Premium | $255 (1.5x) | $1,020 | Fertilizer (~$50) + drying rack time | ~$930 | +$30 |
| Heavenly | $272 (1.6x) | $1,088 | Fertilizer ($50) + drying rack ($300) + Botanist ($200/day) | ~$738 | -$162 (net loss without scale) |

{{< callout "warning" >}}Heavenly quality is NOT worth the extra cost for small-scale operations. The drying rack ($300) + Botanist (if automated, $200/day) eats into your margin. At 4 units per harvest, you lose $162 per harvest compared to Standard. However, at 20+ units per harvest (Barn scale), the drying rack cost is amortized and Heavenly becomes profitable. The community consensus on r/Schedule_I confirms: "There's no real point in Heavenly unless you want the extra 5-6% chance to get a new customer."{{< /callout >}}

{{< insight >}}Cocaine is the most quality-sensitive product in the game. Going from Standard to Premium on Cocaine 8-mix adds $63 per unit in sell price. At 8 units per harvest, that's $504 extra per cycle -- easily worth the fertilizer investment. Meth is the least quality-sensitive -- the $10-20 extra per unit barely covers fertilizer cost.{{< /insight >}}

## Growing Conditions Guide

How each condition affects quality:

| Condition | Effect On Quality | Effect On Yield | Effect On Growth Time | Recommended For |
|-----------|------------------|----------------|---------------------|----------------|
| Fertilizer only | +1 tier (Standard -> Premium) | Normal | Normal | Premium quality |
| Fertilizer + Drying Rack | +2 tiers (Standard -> Heavenly) | Normal | +1 day drying time | Heavenly (large scale) |
| Speed Grow only | No change | Normal | -30% growth time | Fast production (Standard quality) |
| PGR only | -1 tier (-> Standard if Premium base) | +50% yield | Normal | Maximum volume (low quality) |
| Fertilizer + PGR | No change (cancel out) | +50% yield | Normal | Balanced (Standard quality, high yield) |
| Fertilizer + Speed Grow | No change (cancel out) | Normal | -30% growth time | Fast Premium (if you can get both) |

**Source:** Steam Community complete walkthrough guide (March 2025) and Fandom Wiki Quality page.

## Quality Decision Matrix

| Your Situation | Recommended Quality | Reasoning |
|---------------|-------------------|-----------|
| Early game (4 pots), need cash fast | Standard | Quality upgrades cost more than they return at small scale |
| Mid game (8 pots), Bungalow with Botanist | Premium | Fertilizer is cheap ($50), drying rack is optional |
| Late game (Barn, 16+ pots), full automation | Premium on weed, Heavenly on cocaine | Scale amortizes drying rack cost |
| Selling to customers directly | Premium | Higher quality = more customer unlocks, better repeat rates |
| Supplying dealers | Standard or Premium | Dealers don't benefit from quality as much as direct sales |
| Need to unlock a specific customer | Heavenly | The extra 5-6% unlock chance can be worth it for rare customers |

### Practical Example

You have a Barn with 20 pots, running Cocaine 8-mix ($625 base). Your Botanist is already hired ($200/day).

**Standard quality (no fertilizer):**
- 20 units x $875 = $17,500 gross
- $17,500 - $200 (Botanist wage) = $17,300 net

**Premium quality (fertilizer on all 20):**
- 20 units x $938 = $18,760 gross
- $18,760 - $200 (Botanist) - $50 (fertilizer) = $18,510 net

**Heavenly quality (fertilizer + drying rack, 1 day delay):**
- 20 units x $1,000 = $20,000 gross
- $20,000 - $200 (Botanist) - $50 (fertilizer) - $300 (drying rack amortized) = $19,450 net

**Decision:** Go Premium. It adds $1,210 per harvest vs Standard with minimal effort (just apply fertilizer). Heavenly adds another $940 but requires the drying rack purchase ($300) and delays harvest by a day. At Barn scale, the drying rack pays for itself in about 3 harvests.

## Community Questions About Quality

From r/Schedule_I discussions (various threads):

**Q: Does quality matter for dealer sales?**
A: Dealers don't see a significant difference in buyer behavior based on quality. The standard pricing rule applies: dealers sell at the price you set. Quality mainly affects direct customer sales and customer unlock rates.

**Q: What's the point of Heavenly quality?**
A: "Currently the highest required quality is Premium, so there's no real point in Heavenly unless you want the extra 5-6% chance to get a new customer." (Steam Community discussion, April 2025). Heavenly is mainly for completionists and min-maxers.

**Q: Is fertilizer worth the investment?**
A: Yes, at medium-to-large scale. Fertilizer costs $50 and adds roughly 7% to your sell price. At 10+ units per harvest, that's $100-300 extra revenue for a $50 investment. (r/Schedule_I "Is fertilizer worth it" thread)
