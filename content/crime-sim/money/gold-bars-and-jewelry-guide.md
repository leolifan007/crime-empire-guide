Do not sell gold bars for quick cash. I made this mistake on my first 1.3 playthrough and regretted it when I realized gold bars are the currency for hideout basement upgrades -- and those upgrades unlock higher-value contracts that pay back 5x the gold's fence price.

**What changed in 1.3:**

Update 1.3 introduced a parallel economy system:
- Gold bars -- new resource for hideout basement upgrades
- Jewelry loot -- authentic and fake variants
- Basement hideout expansion -- upgradeable with gold bars
- PvP competition modes -- new income with risk
- Master Key -- premium tool for instant lock opening

**Gold bars:**

Found in high-security targets, heist locations, and rare loot boxes. Gold bars are heavy and take multiple inventory slots. They are used exclusively for basement upgrades.

| Upgrade | Gold Bar Cost | Effect |
|---------|--------------|--------|
| Storage Rack | 2 bars | +50% hideout storage capacity |
| Tool Wall | 3 bars | Tool break rate reduced |
| Security Door | 5 bars | Loot safety improved |
| Workshop Bench | 8 bars | Craft better tools |

Each upgrade snowballs: better storage holds more high-value items, better tools mean faster heists, better security protects your stash.

Sell gold bars only if the basement is fully upgraded and you need immediate cash. Even then, the fence price of roughly $800-$1,500 per bar is less valuable than the upgrade ROI over 10+ heists.

Gold bars are fragile to loss. If you get arrested carrying them, they are gone. I stash them in hideout upgrades immediately after each heist.

**Jewelry system:**

Authentic jewelry is found in high-value safes and display cases. Higher fence price (~$400-$800 per piece). Fake jewelry is common in low-security houses, lower price (~$50-$200).

I check items in my inventory to tell them apart -- authentic pieces have a distinctive label or shine effect. If a skill leaflet for Jewelry Appraisal exists, it makes the distinction automatic.

Keep authentic jewelry in your stash for item requests before selling. Some NPCs pay up to $1,000 per piece for authentic requests.

**New income flow I use:**

1. Run standard heists for cash and tools
2. Target high-security houses for gold bars and authentic jewelry
3. Use gold bars for basement upgrades immediately
4. Sell fake jewelry at fence, keep authentic for requests
5. Use upgraded basement to unlock higher-paying contracts
6. Repeat until basement is fully upgraded
7. Sell excess gold bars for cash
