I played most of Crime Simulator solo before trying co-op. The difference is night and day -- with a coordinated team, you clear houses faster and pull in way more loot per run. But without coordination, co-op is chaos.

**The fastest role setup I have used:**

1 Lockpick (entry) + 1 Lookout (perimeter) + 1 Hauler (loot) + 1 Driver (escape). Rotate roles every 2-3 runs so everyone stays flexible.

- **Lockpick:** Best lockpick and hacking tools, opens all doors, handles electronic security
- **Lookout:** Watches patrol routes, monitors police radio, warns team of approaching NPCs
- **Hauler:** Largest inventory capacity, prioritizes high-value items, services item requests
- **Driver:** Stays near exit, parks vehicle for quick escape, handles police evasion

The Driver role is the most underrated. A good driver prevents wipes. When I play Driver, the rest of the team can focus on looting instead of worrying about escape routes.

**Communication that works:**

Before the heist: agree on target, assign roles, check everyone has tools, set a hard extraction limit.

During the heist: call out NPC positions, confirm doors are clear before advancing, call "hot" for police alert and "cold" for all clear.

After the heist: meet at a pre-arranged extraction point. Do not sell loot individually -- pool at the hideout and split later. I have seen teams argue over loot distribution, but pooling avoids this.

**Advanced tactics I have seen work:**

Split and conquer works well with 3+ players. Each player takes a different floor. The Hauler follows the callouts.

Decoy: one player distracts security while the team loots. The decoy creates noise outside, leads police on a chase, retreats when the team signals clear. High risk, but the payoff is a completely unbothered looting experience.

Multi-house chains: once your team is coordinated, chain 3-4 houses in one outing. Hit house A, drop loot at a safe spot, hit house B, repeat. This triples loot-per-run but requires good communication.

**Quota scaling in co-op:**

| Players | Quota Multiplier | Best Approach |
|---------|-----------------|---------------|
| 2 | 1.5x | Pair up per house |
| 3 | 2x | Split + decoy |
| 4 | 2.5x | Full role assignments |

The quota scales but payout scales faster. A coordinated 4-player team can clear 3-4x the loot of the same number playing solo.
