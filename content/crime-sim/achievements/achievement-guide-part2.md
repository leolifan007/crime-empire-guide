These are the achievements that took me real effort to unlock. Some require multiple playthroughs or very specific conditions. I would not attempt them until you have completed Standard mode and understand all the mechanics.

**Challenge achievements (15):**

Challenges are optional objectives that appear during runs. Completing them in specific quantities unlocks these.

| Achievement | Requirement | Difficulty |
|-------------|-------------|------------|
| Insane victory | Complete 5 challenges in one game | Medium |
| Master challenger | Complete 10 challenges in one game | Hard |
| Challenge addict | Complete 25 challenges total | Medium |
| Speed demon | Complete 3 time-based challenges | Hard |
| Silent but deadly | Complete 3 stealth challenges | Medium |
| Heavy hitter | Complete 3 destruction challenges | Medium |
| All-rounder | Complete one of each challenge type | Very Hard |

For "All-rounder," I used a large house with multiple approaches. Ground floor for stealth, basement for destruction, upstairs for time-based tasks. One house can satisfy all challenge types if you plan your route.

**Speed achievements (5):**

These have strict time requirements. I found "Speed runner" the easiest -- choose a small apartment, grab 2 items, exit. Took me about 90 seconds.

| Achievement | Requirement | Strategy |
|-------------|-------------|----------|
| Speed runner | Complete a heist under 2 minutes | Small apartment, grab 2 items, leave |
| Quick getaway | Escape police within 30 seconds | Trigger a minor alert near a hiding spot |
| Speed looter | Steal 5 items in under 1 minute | Pre-scout layout, sprint past everything |
| Rush hour | Complete 3 heists in under 30 minutes | Chain small houses back to back |
| Delivery of a lifetime | Complete item request within 1 minute | Keep requested item ready, accept, deliver |

**Rare condition achievements (10):**

These require unusual circumstances. "Three and free" (escape a 3-star police chase) is easier with the Driver role or speed boost skill. I triggered it by deliberately smashing a window in front of a patrol car, then hiding in a closet until the timer dropped.

"Millionaire" is the grindiest achievement. At the rate I was earning in late game (roughly $2,000-$5,000 per hour), expect 40-60 hours of play. Completionist mode is much faster than quota-constrained runs.

**What I found most useful:**

- Stack achievements in single runs -- one large house can satisfy 3-4 requirements
- Back up saves before attempting risky runs
