Crime Simulator has 60 Steam achievements total. About half of them come naturally through normal gameplay. I got the first 20 without even trying. The rest require specific effort.

Use Completionist mode for achievement hunting. It removes quota pressure and gives you unlimited time. I tried hunting in Standard mode and kept getting interrupted by quota deadlines.

**Key achievement categories:**

| Category | Count | Difficulty |
|----------|-------|------------|
| Tutorial/Introduction | 5 | Very Easy |
| Progression (rank-based) | 10 | Easy |
| Challenges | 15 | Medium-Hard |
| Speed milestones | 5 | Hard |
| Rare conditions | 10 | Very Hard |
| Update 1.3 | 5 | Medium |

The tutorial achievements are automatic. The progression ones come from ranking up naturally -- by the time I hit Rank 4, I had most of them. Challenges and speed achievements are where you actually have to change how you play.

I found the Steam 100% Achievement Guide (linked below) and the TrueSteamAchievements page most useful for tracking my progress. The community guide is a work in progress but has the current known strategies for each achievement.

For the harder half of the list, see [Part 2](/crime-sim/achievements/achievement-guide-part2/).
