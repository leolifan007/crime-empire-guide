I spent my first few Crime Simulator runs using nothing but the crowbar on every door. Loud, slow, and got me caught repeatedly. Better tools changed everything.

Prioritize the lockpick set first. It replaces the noisy crowbar on most locked doors and pays for itself within 2-3 heists.

**Tool overview:**

Crime Simulator has a growing arsenal of tools for breaking in, disabling security, and escaping. Tools break with use and must be repaired or replaced. Choosing the right tool for each job is the difference between a clean score and an arrest.

**Lockpick set** -- The bread and butter of every heist. Lets you open locked doors silently. Without it, you rely on the crowbar, which is loud and alerts nearby NPCs. A simple rotation mini-game: turn the pick left or right until the lock gives. Higher-quality sets handle tough locks better and take longer to break.

**Crowbar** -- Your starter tool. Works on doors, windows, and crates. Loud. Use it only as backup or for breaking into non-essential containers. I stopped carrying a crowbar after getting the basic lockpick set.

**Flashlight** -- Many interiors are dark. Without it, you miss loot in corners and on shelves. A must-have for every run. The pro version has a wider beam and longer battery.

**Sleeping gas** -- Neutralizes a single NPC silently. I use it when a guard is patrolling a room I need to search. One can per NPC. Do not use it near other NPCs -- they will notice.

**Hacking tool** -- Unlocked through the Hacking skill leaflet. Opens electronic door locks and security panels. Without it, electronic locks are impassable. The leaflet drops from special loot boxes.

**Glass knife** -- Cuts glass silently for window entry. Quieter than breaking glass. I use it on houses with alarmed windows.

**Cable ties** -- Restrain subdued NPCs so they do not wake up and raise alarms. I carry a few for larger houses with multiple NPCs.

**Drill** -- Opens safes. Slow and noisy. Use only when the safe content justifies the risk. I check the safe value through the job board intel before carrying a drill.

**Blowtorch** -- Cuts through reinforced doors and gates. Heavy, noisy, slow. Late-game tool for high-security targets.

**My buy order:**

1. Basic lockpick set
2. Flashlight
3. Sleeping gas (1 can)
4. Standard lockpick set (when available)
5. Hacking tool (rush the leaflet hunt)
6. Cable ties (for larger houses)

For upgrade paths and advanced tools, see the [Tool Upgrades guide](/crime-sim/tools/tool-upgrades-and-advanced/).
