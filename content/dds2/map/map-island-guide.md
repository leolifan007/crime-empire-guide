Isla Sombra in DDS2 consists of multiple islands, each with its own characteristics, client base, police density, and available resources. Understanding the map layout is essential for efficient travel, territory planning, and supply chain management. This guide covers every accessible island, key points of interest, and the fastest travel routes between them.

**Island overview:**

Based on the community maps on Miraheze and Kanka, there are 7 major island zones:

| Island | Unlock Order | Best For | Police Density |
|--------|-------------|----------|---------------|
| Small Island | Starting zone | Tutorial, early deliveries, no police | None |
| Archipelago | First expansion | General operations, restaurant district | Low |
| Callejon | Mid-game | Processing, industrial equipment, gang territory | Medium |
| Jungle | Mid-game | Cannabis growing, raw materials | Low |
| Slavs Bay | Late-game | High-end production (meth, cocaine) | High |
| Paraiso Peninsular | Late-game | Endgame operations, high-value clients | High |
| Bahia De Oro | Endgame | Casino DLC area, premium clients | Very High |

The islands are gated by Street Cred level and story progression. You cannot access Callejon until you have completed sufficient missions in Archipelago. Similarly, Slavs Bay and later areas require higher Street Cred.

**Island details and key locations:**

**Small Island (Starting zone):**
- No police presence -- completely safe for beginners
- Key locations: Starting beach, Shady's location, tutorial warehouse
- Available resources: Basic ingredients, limited vendor selection
- Hideouts: No purchasable hideouts -- the starter shack is the only base
- Drug demand: Cannabis and amphetamine only

**Archipelago:**
- Low police presence, good for early expansion
- Key locations: Restaurant (Brisa Marina), Mechanic Ezequiel Ramos (Yellow Car vendor), general stores
- Hideouts: Cottage (~$4k) near dock, restaurant building (~$12k)
- Notice boards: 1 bulletin board north of the restaurant
- Drug demand: Cannabis, amphetamine, meth

**Callejon:**
- Medium police presence, industrial zone with good equipment shops
- Key locations: Hardware store, chemical supplier, vehicle dealer
- Hideouts: Industrial unit (~$6k), warehouse (~$15k)
- Notice boards: 1 bulletin board east of hardware store
- Drug demand: All types except cocaine

**Jungle:**
- Low police presence, excellent for low-visibility operations
- Key locations: General store, farm supply, pawn shop
- Hideouts: Cabin (~$3k), river house (~$10k)
- Drug demand: Cannabis and amphetamine primarily
- Best for: Raw material growing and low-heat production

**Slavs Bay:**
- High police presence, high potential profits
- Key locations: Coastal properties, underground locations
- Hideouts: Coastal house (~$8k), underground lab (~$20k)
- Drug demand: All types including high-end products

**Fast travel routes:**

The fastest method to cross between islands is by boat. However, some routes have shortcuts:

| Route | Method | Time | Risk |
|-------|--------|------|------|
| Small Island to Archipelago | Boat (north passage) | 2-3 minutes | Low |
| Archipelago to Callejon | Boat (west route) | 3-4 minutes | Low |
| Callejon to Jungle | Boat (south route) | 4-5 minutes | Medium (patrols) |
| Jungle to Slavs Bay | Boat (south-east) | 5-7 minutes | Medium |
| Callejon to Slavs Bay | Boat (cross-island) | 6-8 minutes | High |
| Any to Paraiso Peninsular | Boat (requires high Street Cred unlock) | 5-10 minutes | High |

{{< callout "tip" >}}
The best early-game hideout location is the cottage on the island between Callejon and Archipelago. The Reddit community recommends this location because it provides easy access to both zones, has a dock for boat parking, and has moderate visibility that is manageable with basic precautions.
{{< /callout >}}

**Key Points of Interest (POIs):**

| POI Type | Islands | Purpose |
|----------|---------|---------|
| Gas stations | All major islands | Vehicle refueling |
| Hardware stores | Callejon, Archipelago | Lab equipment purchases |
| Pharmacies | All islands | Precursor chemicals |
| General stores | All islands | Basic supplies, cigarettes, food |
| Notice boards | 19 total across all islands | Employee hiring, side tasks |
| Dead drop locations | Multiple per island | Customer order drop-off points |
| Mechanic shops | Archipelago, Callejon | Vehicle purchases and upgrades |
| Docks | All islands | Boat parking and fuel |

**Practical example: Efficient supply chain route**

Here is the supply chain route I use that minimizes travel time:

1. **Jungle hideout** -- Cannabis grow and raw material processing
2. **Boat to Callejon** (4 minutes) -- Transport raw materials to processing hub
3. **Callejon warehouse** -- Amphetamine and meth production
4. **Boat to Archipelago** (3 minutes) -- Transport finished product to sales hub
5. **Archipelago hideout** -- Finished product storage and dealer distribution
6. **Small Island** (2 minutes from Archipelago) -- Secondary sales territory

This circular route touches all key zones in approximately 15 minutes of travel time per loop. With a fast boat and planned stops, you can complete a full supply chain cycle in under an hour.
