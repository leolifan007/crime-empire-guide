Your first hour on Isla Sombra is about survival, not profits. I learned this the hard way -- my first run I focused on cooking and ended up broke with no clients.

Complete the tutorial missions, meet your contact Shady, secure your first hideout, and make deliveries until you hit $5,000. Cooking comes after you have a reliable client pipeline.

**Your first steps:**

You wash up on a beach with nothing. Head to the marker on your minimap to meet Shady, your first contact. He gives you a burner phone with the ShadyComm app, your first 10g of low-grade product, and 3 client contacts.

ShadyComm is your lifeline -- all orders come through this app. I check it every 10 minutes. Missing orders hurts your reputation and slows early cash flow.

**Deliveries ($0 to $2,000):**

Open ShadyComm and accept delivery jobs closest to your position. Each pays $80-150. Clear all orders in your zone, plot a circular route (closest first), complete them, return for new batch.

The Steam quick-start guide notes that police hours bring extra cash. I try to keep product moving during these windows for the payout bonus.

**Set up your first lab:**

Once you have $2,000, buy a basic lab kit and find a recipe card. The first recipe (basic meth or cannabis) unlocks after finding the recipe card in the tutorial zone warehouse.

Do not guess recipe ratios. I wasted my first batch of materials guessing. Basic meth starts with a 5:3:2 ratio (precursor : solvent : catalyst) from what the data mine shows.

**Buy a second hideout:**

After hitting $5,000, buy a second hideout. Community guides on Reddit consistently recommend this before upgrading gear. One raid can wipe a single-hideout player out completely.

| Priority | Item | Cost | Why |
|----------|------|------|-----|
| 1 | Second hideout | $1,500-4,500 | Raid protection, split risk |
| 2 | Bike | $400 | 3x faster than running |
| 3 | Better backpack | $250 | Doubles carry capacity |
| 4 | Lockpick kit (tier 2) | $150 | Access better loot spots |

**The most common beginner trap:**

Thinking the game is about cooking. It is not. The first 2 hours are about building your client network and territory. More clients = bigger orders = faster cash. Cooking scales after you have reliable distribution. Multiple Reddit threads confirm this strategy.
