Drug Dealer Simulator 2 has 39 Steam achievements (confirmed on the official Steam page). Achievement tracking sites like TrueAchievements and Steam Hunters document the full list. I used these sites along with the community 100% guide to figure out the unlock conditions.

**Story and progression achievements:**

| Achievement | Unlock Condition | Unlock Rate (Steam) |
|-------------|-----------------|-------------------|
| I Am The One Who Knocks | Achieve Street Cred level 4 | Common |
| Higher learning | Progress through story | Common |
| The Kingpin | Reach max Street Cred | Rare |
| Side hustle | Complete side missions | ~0.6% (rare) |

**Collectible and misc achievements:**

| Achievement | Unlock Condition | Unlock Rate |
|-------------|-----------------|-------------|
| Environmentalist | Sell 100 glass bottles | ~12.6% (most common misc) |
| Never let you down | Unknown trigger | Rare |
| 500 CIGARETTES | Sell 500 cigarettes | ~0.7% (very rare) |
| A warm place | Unknown trigger | Rare |
| Home sweet home | Unknown trigger | - |

**DLC/update achievements:**

The Casino DLC adds additional achievements. I have one on my list involving killing the casino boss with a bomb and paying B4,000,000 afterward based on the Steam community 100% guide.

**Rarity analysis:**

Based on Steam Hunters data, the rarest achievements have under 1% unlock rates (Side hustle at 0.6%, 500 CIGARETTES at 0.7%). The most common misc achievement is Environmentalist at 12.6%. This suggests the game rewards dedicated play over basic progression.

**How to track:**

| Method | How |
|--------|-----|
| In-game | Steam overlay (Shift+Tab) -> Achievements |
| Steam Community | steamcommunity.com/app/1708850/achievements |
| Third-party | TrueAchievements, Steam Hunters, PlayTracker |

For the hidden achievements, see [Hidden and Secret Achievements](/dds2/achievements/hidden-achievements/).
