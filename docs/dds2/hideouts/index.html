DDS2 Hideout Locations Guide and Raid Protection

Jun 9, 2026

Hideouts in DDS2 can be raided based on your wanted level and the hideout's visibility. I lost my first major stockpile to a raid because I kept everything in the starter shack. The community guide "Pedro Alvarez Real Estate Agency" documents all locations with prices, water/phone line availability, and visibility ratings.

How raids work:

The Fandom hideout page confirms: raid probability is affected by wanted level (None / Person of Interest / Suspect / Wanted) and hideout visibility. Helicopters can appear during raids.

Wanted Level Raid Probability Protection Strategy
None Low Standard precautions
Person of Interest Medium Split assets, 2+ hideouts
Suspect High Guards, hidden compartments
Wanted Very High Minimize time at any hideout

Hideout purchase order I use:

From cross-referencing the community hideout guide and Reddit discussions:

Purchase # Location Type Estimated Cost Purpose
Starter Beach shack Free Tutorial, early storage
2nd Rural/jungle ~$1,500-4,500 Raw materials, low visibility
3rd Industrial/Callejon ~$5,000-8,000 Processing, medium visibility
4th Coastal/Slavs Bay ~$8,000-15,000 High-value production
5th+ Downtown/villas $15,000+ Endgame, co-op hub

Community consensus from Reddit: if you turn off raids (game settings), you can use any location for space. With raids enabled, prioritize low-visibility locations for your main lab.

Protection checklist:

  • Own at least 2 hideouts before your net worth exceeds $5,000
  • Keep raw materials and finished product in separate locations
  • Maintain low wanted level before visiting high-value hideouts
  • Use rural/jungle hideouts for your main lab (lower visibility)
  • When raided, escape using parkour rather than fighting

DDS2 Raid Prevention: Protecting Your Assets

Jun 9, 2026

Getting raided in DDS2 is preventable. I know because I have been raided several times and each time I could trace it back to something I did wrong. The game's raid system checks wanted level and hideout visibility. Keep both low and your assets stay safe.

Raid cause and effect:

Factor Impact on Raid Chance How to Control
Wanted level Direct multiplier Avoid illegal activity in populated areas
Hideout visibility Location-dependent Choose rural/jungle hideouts for labs
Product volume Indirect (attracts attention) Split across locations
Guard presence Reduces probability Hire guards when available

Three-hideout model (community standard I use):

Hideout Stores Risk if Raided
A (rural) Raw materials, low-value product Low -- easy to replace
B (industrial) Processing equipment, work-in-progress Medium -- loss of time
C (coastal) Finished product, cash High -- biggest value

If one hideout gets raided, you lose at most one-third of your operation. I learned this from losing a full stash in the starter shack.

What to do during a raid:

Per the Fandom wiki: raids can involve helicopters and heavily armed police. My options ranked:

  1. Escape -- use parkour to break line of sight
  2. Hide -- some hideouts have hiding spots
  3. Fight -- not recommended, police arrive in waves
  4. Accept loss -- if assets are split, damage is contained

Community consensus: fighting during a raid increases your wanted level and makes future raids more likely. Escape is always the better option.

Post-raid recovery:

Step Action Timeframe
1 Check which hideout was hit Immediate
2 Transfer remaining assets to safe locations Within 1 hour
3 Reduce wanted level (lay low, avoid crime) 1-3 in-game days
4 Rebuild stock at alternate lab 3-5 in-game days
