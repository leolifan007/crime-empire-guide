DDS2 Achievement Guide: Full List (39 Achievements Verified)

Jun 9, 2026

Tracking sites like TrueAchievements, Steam Hunters, and PlayTracker document the full list with global unlock percentages. I used these sites along with the Steam Community 100% achievement guide to compile accurate unlock conditions.

Practical example: 100% completion strategy

Based on the Steam community "EN Achievements 100%" guide and my own testing:

  1. Story first -- Progress through the main questline. This unlocks the base progression achievements naturally. Do not skip side missions during this phase.
  2. Street Cred grind -- "I Am The One Who Knocks" requires level 4. Beat gang members in each territory, complete influencer tasks, and avoid getting arrested. This typically takes 10-15 hours of focused play.
  3. Grind milestones -- For "Environmentalist," collect every glass bottle you find from the first hour. For "500 CIGARETTES," buy cigarettes from vendors whenever available and sell them through your dealer network. These are long-term grinds that require consistent effort.
  4. Combat achievements -- "No buddy I am not lost" requires killing 50 thugs. Equip melee weapons for stealthier takedowns. Gang members respawn in fixed locations, so you can farm them systematically.
  5. DLC content -- The Casino DLC has its own achievement set including casino-specific missions and boss encounters. The 100% achievement guide covers these in detail.

Rarity analysis:

Based on Steam Hunters data, the rarest achievements have under 1% unlock rates. "Side hustle" at 0.6% and "500 CIGARETTES" at 0.7% are the hardest base-game achievements. The most common misc achievement is "Environmentalist" at 12.6%. This distribution suggests the game rewards dedicated play over basic progression. Many players complete the story but do not invest the additional 30-40 hours needed for grind achievements.

Practical example: How to unlock "500 CIGARETTES" efficiently

This is one of the grindiest achievements in the game. Here is the most efficient approach:

  1. Check every general store and vendor for cigarette stock. Key locations include the stores in Callejon, Jungle, and Archipelago.
  2. Buy all available cigarettes whenever you visit a vendor. Do not skip this even if you have other purchases planned.
  3. Store cigarettes in a dedicated hideout that you use as a supply hub. Do not mix them with other products.
  4. Assign a dealer to sell cigarettes in a high-traffic territory. Cigarettes have fast turnover because the demand exists in multiple islands.
  5. Track your progress through the Steam overlay. The achievement triggers at 500 units sold, not purchased.

At approximately 10-20 cigarettes available per store visit, expect 25-50 vendor trips minimum. This achievement takes most players 30-40 hours of gameplay.

100% completion strategy:

Based on the Steam community "EN Achievements 100%" guide and cross-referencing with the Casino DLC guide:

  1. Story first -- Progress through the main questline to unlock base achievements. This includes "You're hired!" (hire first employee) and story-related progression.
  2. Street Cred grind -- "I Am The One Who Knocks" (level 4) and "The Kingpin" (max level). Beat gang members and complete influencer missions.
  3. Misc collectibles -- Start collecting glass bottles, cigarettes, and palm leaves from hour one. Do not wait until you need them.
  4. Combat achievements -- "No buddy I am not lost" (50 thugs). Gang member spawn locations are consistent across restarts.
  5. Casino DLC -- Complete after the base game. The DLC has its own story missions and boss fights.
