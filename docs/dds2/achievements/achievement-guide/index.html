DDS2 Achievement Guide: Full List (39 Achievements Verified)

Updated: Jun 9, 2026

Drug Dealer Simulator 2 has 39 Steam achievements as confirmed on the official Steam page. The base game contains 21 achievements, and there are 2 DLC packs that add 18 more. Tracking sites like TrueAchievements, Steam Hunters, and PlayTracker document the full list with global unlock percentages. I used these sites along with the Steam Community 100% achievement guide to compile accurate unlock conditions.

DLC/update achievements:

The Casino DLC (released around June 2025) adds a significant number of additional achievements. Based on the Steam Community 100% guide, one DLC achievement involves killing the casino boss with a bomb and paying B4,000,000 afterward. There are Casino-related achievements requiring completing casino missions. The DDS2 roadmap from the developers mentioned continued content updates.

Practical example: 100% completion strategy

Based on the Steam community "EN Achievements 100%" guide and my own testing:

  1. Story first -- Progress through the main questline. This unlocks the base progression achievements naturally. Do not skip side missions during this phase.
  2. Street Cred grind -- "I Am The One Who Knocks" requires level 4. Beat gang members in each territory, complete influencer tasks, and avoid getting arrested. This typically takes 10-15 hours of focused play.
  3. Grind milestones -- For "Environmentalist," collect every glass bottle you find from the first hour. For "500 CIGARETTES," buy cigarettes from vendors whenever available and sell them through your dealer network. These are long-term grinds that require consistent effort.
  4. Combat achievements -- "No buddy I am not lost" requires killing 50 thugs. Equip melee weapons for stealthier takedowns. Gang members respawn in fixed locations, so you can farm them systematically.
  5. DLC content -- The Casino DLC has its own achievement set including casino-specific missions and boss encounters. The 100% achievement guide covers these in detail.

Rarity analysis:

Based on Steam Hunters data, the rarest achievements have under 1% unlock rates. "Side hustle" at 0.6% and "500 CIGARETTES" at 0.7% are the hardest base-game achievements. The most common misc achievement is "Environmentalist" at 12.6%. This distribution suggests the game rewards dedicated play over basic progression. Many players complete the story but do not invest the additional 30-40 hours needed for grind achievements.
