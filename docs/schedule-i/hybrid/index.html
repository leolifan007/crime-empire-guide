Top 10 Community Custom Strains By Profit: Ranked With Verified Ingredient Lists

Jun 18, 2026
Short answer: The most profitable community-discovered custom strain is the Cocaine 9-mix ("God Mix") at ~$717 per unit. For weed-based strains, "Grandmaster Kush" (OG Kush + Horse Semen + Mega Bean + Gasoline) sells for ~$165-175 per unit with the lowest ingredient cost among top-tier strains. Below is a ranked list of 10 community-verified strains.

This is a curated ranking of real community-discovered strains -- not a calculator tool. Each entry includes ingredient list, sell price, profit margin, and source verification. Data cross-referenced from r/Schedule_I highest profit strains thread (May 2025), PC Gamer mixing recipes guide (April 2026), and Steam Community mixing database (January 2026).

The margin percentages reveal a counter-intuitive truth: weed-based strains (Ocean OG at 1,054% margin) have WAY higher profit margins than cocaine strains (God Mix at 219%). But the absolute profit per unit is much lower. The decision comes down to: do you want high volume with low complexity (weed) or low volume with high per-unit profit (cocaine)? For automated production, weed wins on throughput. For dealer-limited setups, cocaine wins.

Practical Example

You have a Bungalow with 2 Mixing Stations and want to maximize profit with a 4-ingredient recipe.

Option A: Grandmaster Kush (OG Kush + Horse Semen + Mega Bean + Gasoline)

  • 2 stations, 4 ingredients
  • $170 sell price, ~$17 cost
  • $153 profit per unit
  • 8-12 units/day with Bungalow automation
  • Daily profit: ~$1,224-1,836

Option B: Cocaine 2-mix

  • 2 stations, 2 ingredients
  • ~$120 sell price, ~$70 cost (coca seeds are expensive)
  • $50 profit per unit
  • 8-12 units/day
  • Daily profit: ~$400-600

Winner: Grandmaster Kush makes roughly 3x more profit per day with the same setup. Cocaine becomes better only when you have 5+ stations (Barn/Warehouse scale).

Practical Example

You're at Hustler II with $20,000 saved. You have the Bungalow, 2 Mixing Stations, and access to Albert Hoover (Delivery unlocked) and the Motel NPC for Horse Semen and Mega Bean.

Current situation: You're making Ocean OG ($150/unit) with Cuke, Motor Oil, and Battery.

Optimization: Switch to Grandmaster Kush (OG Kush + Horse Semen + Mega Bean + Gasoline).

Why:

  • Same 2 stations, same 4 ingredients
  • Sell price jumps from $150 to $170 (13% increase)
  • Horse Semen ($20) + Mega Bean ($25) + Gasoline ($12) = $57 ingredient cost per batch
  • But you need only 1 of each per mix, and each batch creates multiple units
  • Net profit per unit increases from $137 to ~$153

Result: ~$90 extra per harvest cycle with zero additional equipment cost. Over 10 harvest cycles: ~$900 extra profit.
