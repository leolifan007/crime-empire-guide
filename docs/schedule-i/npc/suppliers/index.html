Fastest Supplier Unlock Route: From Street Rat to Kingpin in Minimum Orders

Updated: Jun 18, 2026
Short answer: Unlock Albert Hoover immediately (available from start). Befriend Elizabeth Homley or Kevin Oakley to unlock Shirley Watts and Fungal Phil simultaneously. Reach higher ranks for Salvador Moreno. The fastest route: place Dead Drop orders for the cheapest items first to build relationship, then scale up. Minimum total cost to unlock all four: roughly $8,000 in Dead Drop orders plus rank progression. If you're still short on cash, our $10K in First Hour guide covers how to raise capital fast.

This is an optimized unlock route -- not a list of who the suppliers are. Follow this order and you'll unlock delivery (the max relationship perk) faster than random ordering.

Step-by-Step Route

Step 1: Albert Hoover (Northtown) -- Available from Start

Albert is the only supplier you can access immediately. No connection needed.

Fastest path to Delivery:

  1. Place 3 Dead Drop orders of 1-2 OG Kush seeds each ($10 per seed). Delivery takes ~30 min per item.
  2. Pay the debt immediately at the stash behind Dan's Hardware (alley north of Sauerkraut Supreme Pizza).
  3. After 3-4 orders, Albert reaches Friendly (already default) and moves to Loyal.
  4. Continue ordering. After approximately 8-10 total orders, Albert unlocks Delivery (order directly to your location via the Delivery App).

Albert's best products by cost efficiency:

Product Cost Sell Value Per Unit Profit Margin Best For
OG Kush seed $10 ~$30 (raw bud) 200% Early game
Green Crack seed $20 ~$40 100% Mid mix recipes
Sour Diesel seed $35 ~$55 57% Premium strains
Always order the cheapest item (OG Kush seeds at $10) for relationship building. Ordering expensive items doesn't speed up relationship gain -- only the number of Dead Drop orders matters, not the value of items ordered. You save money by buying cheap items for relationship and expensive items for production once Delivery is unlocked.

Step 2: Shirley Watts + Fungal Phil (Unlock Together)

These two share the same unlock condition. This is the most efficient unlock in the game because you get two suppliers for one relationship grind.

Pre-work (before unlocking):

  1. Find Elizabeth Homley (motel area) or Kevin Oakley (near Westville).
  2. Sell to them until they reach Friendly status.
  3. This typically takes 3-4 sales of weed or meth.
  4. Once Friendly, Shirley Watts and Fungal Phil appear in your contacts.

Shirley Watts Stash: West face of the Warehouse (south of motel, past the bridge). Fungal Phil Stash: Behind the central Gas-Mart in Downtown.

Optimization tip: Alternate Dead Drop orders between Shirley and Fungal Phil. Each order to one builds relationship for that supplier only, but since you unlocked both at the same time, you can build both relationships in parallel. Place 2 orders to Shirley, then 2 to Fungal Phil, repeat.

Supplier Key Product Cost Best Use
Shirley Watts Pseudo $70 per unit Meth production base ingredient
Fungal Phil Shroom spores ~$50 Mushroom production (added in recent update)

Step 3: Salvador Moreno (Docks) -- Late Game

Salvador unlocks at higher rank (around Enforcer I) and requires connections from your progression.

Stash Location: Behind the church on the east side of the map.

Key product: Coca Plant Seeds ($50-80 per seed) - essential for cocaine production.

Fastest path: Once unlocked, place 5-6 Dead Drop orders of 2 coca seeds each. The seeds are expensive but necessary. Build relationship to Loyal for Meet Up (4 possible locations: under north overpass, under west bridge, west construction site, next to Mayor's house). Max relationship unlocks Delivery.

Salvador's cost efficiency:

Product Cost Sell Value Per Unit Profit Margin Best For
Coca seed $50-80 ~$200 (processed cocaine) 150-300% Mid-late game profit

Practical Example

You're at Hoodlum I rank with $2,000 saved. Your goal is to unlock Shirley Watts and Fungal Phil in 3 in-game days.

Day 1: Find Elizabeth Homley. Sell her 3 batches of OG Kush (total ~$90-120 value). She reaches Friendly. Shirley Watts and Fungal Phil appear in contacts.

Day 2: Place Dead Drop orders: 3 Pseudo from Shirley ($210 total), 3 Spores from Fungal Phil ($150 total). Pay debt at both stashes.

Day 3: Repeat Day 2 orders. Both suppliers now at Friendly-Loyal threshold. Proceed to scale up production.

Total cost: $720 in Dead Drop orders + $90-120 in sampling costs = ~$840. Both suppliers unlocked and at Friendly status in 3 days.

Dead Drop vs Meet Up vs Delivery

Feature Dead Drop Meet Up (Loyal) Delivery (Max)
Order limit 10 items No limit No limit
Delivery time 30 min per item Instant (meet in person) ~15 min per item
Builds relationship? Yes No No
Best for Relationship building Emergency bulk orders Regular production supply
Risk None Must travel to meet location None

The smartest play: build all suppliers to Delivery using cheap Dead Drop orders, then switch to Delivery for your regular supply. This takes about 12-15 Dead Drop orders per supplier (roughly $1,500-2,000 per supplier), but once done, you never have to travel to a stash again. The Delivery App sends supplies directly to your location.
