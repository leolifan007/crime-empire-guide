Schedule I Endgame Guide: What To Do After Full Automation

Updated: Jun 18, 2026
Short answer: After full automation, endgame goals include: owning all 6 properties ($430,000 total), unlocking all 6 dealers ($15,500 total buy-in), maxing all 4 suppliers to Delivery status, hitting Kingpin V rank, and building a mega-factory using all properties. Each goal takes roughly 30-60 additional in-game hours.

This guide covers endgame activities after your automation is running. Verified against r/Schedule_I endgame discussions, Steam Community forum posts, and ScreenRant's endgame guide.

Goal 1: Max All Supplier Relationships

This is the highest-impact endgame goal. Once all 4 suppliers reach Delivery status, your supply chain becomes fully passive.

Current status: You probably have Albert Hoover at Delivery and the other 3 at Friendly-Loyal.

To finish:

  • Shirley Watts: 7-10 more Dead Drop orders ($700-1,000)
  • Fungal Phil: 7-10 more Dead Drop orders ($500-700)
  • Salvador Moreno: 10-13 more Dead Drop orders ($1,000-1,600)

Strategy: Place Dead Drop orders during downtime (when your automation is running and you have nothing to do). Follow the supplier unlock route if you're not maxed yet -- it minimizes wasted orders. Order the cheapest item from each. Pay debt immediately. Repeat every in-game day until done.

Goal 2: Unlock All 6 Dealers

At endgame, you should have all 6 dealers active. Cumulative buy-in cost: $15,500.

Dealer Buy-In Unlock Rank Status Check
Benji Coleman $500 Tutorial Should be active
Molly Presley $1,000 Hoodlum I Should be active
Brad Crosby $2,000 Hustler I Should be active
Jane Lucero $3,000 Enforcer I Possibly active
Wei Long $4,000 Block Boss I Usually active at endgame
Leo Rivers $5,000 Kingpin I Endgame unlock
At endgame, you have enough production capacity to supply all 6 dealers. The key is product assignment: give each dealer a product type that matches their customer base. Don't give all dealers the same product -- you'll saturate the market and some dealers will hold stale inventory for 3+ days. Mix weed, meth, and cocaine across different dealers for maximum throughput.

Goal 3: The Mega-Factory (Using All Properties)

The ultimate endgame build: using ALL properties as a connected production empire.

Recommended property assignments:

Property Role Setup Cost Production Focus Employees
Bungalow Seed-to-weed farm ~$3,500 OG Kush + Green Crack growing 4
Barn Mixed production ~$12,000 Weed 4-mix + Meth 6-mix 10
Docks Warehouse Cocaine HQ ~$25,000 Cocaine 8-9 mix 14
Motel Quick-escape / overflow $500 Emergency stash 0
Suburbia House Supply relay $2,000 Ingredient buffer 0
Hyland Manor (optional) Mega-factory ~$250,000 Everything maxed 16+

Production flow:

  1. Bungalow grows base herbs (weed seeds from Albert)
  2. Barn runs weed and meth production lines
  3. Warehouse runs cocaine production
  4. All products flow to dealers or direct sales
  5. Manor acts as central storage + overflow production

Daily profit at this scale: $60,000-100,000/day (gross), $45,000-80,000/day (net after wages).

Goal 4: Optimization Challenges

Once your empire is running, optimize for these community challenges:

Challenge 1: 100% AFK Operation Goal: Make your automation run for 10+ in-game days without any player intervention. Requires: All suppliers at Delivery, large shelf buffers (15+ units per ingredient), all dealers active, and overflow storage.

Challenge 2: Max Profit Per In-Game Hour Current record (per community reports): ~$400/hr with optimized Cocaine 9-mix in Manor mega-factory. Goal: Match or exceed this with your setup.

Challenge 3: Full Customer Unlock There are customers in Schedule I that only unlock at specific conditions (rank, product type, total sales volume). The endgame goal is to unlock every customer by systematically selling every product type at every quality level.

Challenge 4: Speedrun Kingpin V Goal: Reach Kingpin V rank in minimum in-game time. Current community estimates: ~15 in-game hours with optimal path.
