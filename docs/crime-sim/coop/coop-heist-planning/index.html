Crime Simulator Co-op Heist Planning: From Setup to Extraction

Updated: Jun 9, 2026

The best co-op heists I have been on were planned before anyone left the hideout. The team I run with spends 5 minutes planning and 10 minutes executing. Those 5 minutes save us from 20 minutes of prison time.

Before leaving the hideout, we confirm:

  • Target selected from job board or free-roam
  • Security level assessed (locks, NPCs, alarms)
  • Required tools confirmed for each team member
  • Entry point identified (front, back, roof, window)
  • Extraction point agreed (vehicle, alley, rooftop)
  • Role assignments confirmed

Target selection:

Not all houses are worth hitting in co-op. The payout from a single large house split 4 ways is sometimes less than running two medium houses simultaneously.

I prioritize houses with:

  • Multiple floors (each player takes a floor)
  • Electronic locks (needs the Hacker to handle while others loot)
  • Confirmed high-value items from job board intel

Gear prep checklist:

Each player should carry:

  • Primary tool for their role (lockpick, hacking tool, etc.)
  • Backup tool (crowbar for emergencies)
  • Sleeping gas (neutralize 1 NPC silently)
  • Flashlight (mandatory -- too many dark corners)

Missing tools waste time on site. We double-check inventory before leaving the hideout. I have had runs where someone forgot their lockpick and we had to use the noisy crowbar on every door.

The rotation I have found most efficient:

  1. Scout entry point (15 seconds)
  2. Lockpick entry (30 seconds)
  3. Clear NPCs (2 minutes)
  4. Fan out and loot by priority (5 minutes)
  5. Call extraction (30 seconds)
  6. Fence and split (5 minutes)

About 12-15 minutes per high-value house with a practiced team.
