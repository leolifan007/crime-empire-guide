Crime Simulator Gold Bars and Jewelry Economy (Update 1.3)

Updated: Jun 9, 2026

Do not sell gold bars for quick cash. I made this mistake on my first 1.3 playthrough and regretted it when I realized gold bars are the currency for hideout basement upgrades -- and those upgrades unlock higher-value contracts that pay back 5x the gold's fence price. The safe houses guide has a full breakdown of what each upgrade costs.

What Changed in Update 1.3

Update 1.3 introduced a parallel economy system that fundamentally changes how you should think about loot:

  • Gold bars -- new resource for hideout basement upgrades
  • Jewelry loot -- authentic and fake variants with different values
  • Basement hideout expansion -- upgradeable with gold bars
  • PvP competition modes -- new income stream with risk. For consistent early cash before diving into PvP, the fast money guide outlines the safest routes.
  • Master Key -- premium tool for instant lock opening
  • New loot locations with expanded map areas

Gold Bars: The Basement Currency

Found in high-security targets, heist locations, and rare loot boxes. Gold bars are heavy and take multiple inventory slots. They are used exclusively for basement upgrades.

Gold Bar Basement Upgrades

Upgrade Gold Bar Cost Effect ROI Estimate
Storage Rack 2 bars +50% hideout storage capacity Pays for itself in 5 runs
Tool Wall 3 bars Tool break rate reduced Saves ~$200/run on repairs
Security Door 5 bars Loot safety improved Prevents total loss on arrest
Workshop Bench 8 bars Craft better tools Unlocks premium tool crafting

Each upgrade snowballs: better storage holds more high-value items, better tools mean faster heists, better security protects your stash.

Purchase upgrades in order: Storage Rack first (more storage means more high-value loot held for requests), then Tool Wall (reduced break rate saves money), then Security Door (protects your stash), and Workshop Bench last. Total investment: 18 gold bars for the full basement.

Should You Ever Sell Gold Bars?

Sell gold bars only if the basement is fully upgraded and you need immediate cash. Even then, the fence price of roughly $800-$1,500 per bar is less valuable than the upgrade ROI over 10+ heists.

Scenario Sell Gold Bar? Reasoning
Basement not upgraded Never Bar is worth 5x more as upgrade currency
Partially upgraded Only if quota is about to fail Otherwise stash for next upgrade
Fully upgraded Yes, if you need cash Fence price is pure profit at this point

Gold Bar Safety

Gold bars are fragile to loss. If you get arrested carrying them, they are gone. I stash them in hideout upgrades immediately after each heist. Never travel between districts with gold bars in your inventory -- go straight home after finding one.

Jewelry System

Authentic jewelry is found in high-value safes and display cases. It has a higher fence price (approximately $400-$800 per piece). Fake jewelry is common in low-security houses with a lower price (approximately $50-$200).

How to Tell Authentic from Fake

Feature Authentic Fake
Fence price $400-$800 $50-$200
Found in High-value safes, display cases Low-security houses, drawers
Description label Has shine/quality indicator Generic description
Item request value Up to $1,000 Rarely requested
Skill leaflet needed? No, but Appraisal helps No

I check items in my inventory to tell them apart -- authentic pieces have a distinctive label or shine effect. If a skill leaflet for Jewelry Appraisal exists, it makes the distinction automatic.

The Jewelry Appraisal skill leaflet is worth hunting for if you frequently hit high-security houses. Without it, you have to manually inspect each jewelry piece. With it, the game highlights authentic pieces in your inventory automatically. I found my copy in a display case in a suburban house with electronic locks.

Jewelry Selling Strategy

Keep authentic jewelry in your stash for item requests before selling. Some NPCs pay up to $1,000 per piece for authentic requests. Fake jewelry should be fenced immediately -- it rarely appears in request lists and takes up inventory space that could hold electronics.

Master Key (Update 1.3)

The Master Key is a premium tool that opens most standard residential locks instantly without a mini-game. It does not work on electronic locks or high-security safes. I use it on every standard door.

Feature Standard Lockpick Master Key
Unlock time 5-15 seconds (mini-game) Instant
Noise level Silent Silent
Works on Standard locks Standard locks
Break rate Moderate Very low
Cost $300-$800 Premium (1.3 content)
Skill leaflet required No (basic tier) No

Combined with the hacking tool for electronic locks, the Master Key covers approximately 80% of entry points. It is the best late-game tool investment I have unlocked.

New Income Flow

  1. Run standard heists for cash and tools
  2. Target high-security houses for gold bars and authentic jewelry
  3. Use gold bars for basement upgrades immediately
  4. Sell fake jewelry at fence, keep authentic for requests
  5. Use upgraded basement to unlock higher-paying contracts
  6. Repeat until basement is fully upgraded
  7. Sell excess gold bars for cash
PvP competition modes (also added in 1.3) provide an alternative income stream if you have good tools and skills. The risk is higher -- you can lose your carried items if defeated -- but the payout per match can exceed a medium house heist. I use PvP as a change of pace, not a primary income source.
