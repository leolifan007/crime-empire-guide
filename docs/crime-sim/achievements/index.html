Crime Simulator Achievement Guide Part 1: Easy to Medium Achievements

Jun 18, 2026

Crime Simulator has 60 Steam achievements total. About half come naturally through normal gameplay -- you will get the first 15-20 without trying. The rest require specific strategies. This guide covers the first 32 achievements (Very Easy to Medium difficulty).

Use Completionist mode for achievement hunting. It removes quota pressure and gives unlimited time. In Standard mode, quota deadlines interrupt focused achievement runs. Switch modes from the main menu before starting a new game.

Tutorial Achievements (Auto-Unlock)

These five achievements unlock during the first few minutes. You cannot miss them.

Achievement Requirement Unlock Rate
Understood? Complete the tutorial 96%
Whoops Die for the first time 77%
Yeah, I can do that Complete 5 jobs 74%
Money maker Earn $5,000 total 72%
Gimme credits Earn 1,000 credits in one game 71%

"Understood?" unlocks as soon as the tutorial jail-break sequence ends. "Whoops" requires your first death -- jump off a second-story balcony if you want it fast.

**Money maker** and **Gimme credits** will unlock naturally in your first 30 minutes. Credits are earned by completing jobs (heist missions). Focus on the tool shop job first -- it pays 200 credits and is available immediately.

Rank Progression Achievements

Ranking up is the core progression loop. The rank determines which tools, zones, and jobs are available.

Achievement Requirement Time to Unlock
Apprentice Reach Rank 4 ~30 min
Professional Reach Rank 8 ~1 hr
Expert Reach Rank 12 ~2 hr
Master Reach Rank 16 ~3 hr
World Class Reach Rank 20 ~5 hr
Legendary Reach Rank 25 ~8 hr
Fastest rank-up strategy: focus on completing item request lists. See the Getting Started guide for a full walkthrough of the first 30 minutes. Each completed list gives roughly 2x the rank XP of a normal job. Check the request board at your hideout before every run and prioritize those items.

Rank 4 (Apprentice) unlocks the tool upgrade shop. Rank 8 (Professional) unlocks a second job district. Rank 16 (Master) gives access to alarm-disabling tools.

Loot & Money Achievements

Achievement Requirement Strategy
Appretince thief Steal 25 items in one game Hit 2-3 small houses -- grab everything
Master thief Steal 100 items in one game Dedicated run, hit 6+ houses
Hoarder $25,000 worth of loot in hideout Save high-value items, dont sell early
Golden card Complete 3 heists in one game Focus on heist targets on map
Clock is ticking Complete 2 timed challenges Timed challenges appear after Rank 6
Sneaky Complete a job without triggering any alarm Use silenced tools on cameras first
Collector Own 1 of each tool type at hideout Buy all tool tiers once unlocked

**Hoarder** is the most delayed of this group. The requirement is $25K total loot value stored at your hideout (not cash). Focus on electronics (laptops, tablets) and jewelry -- they have the best value-to-slot ratio. The [loot locations guide](/crime-sim/loot/loot-locations-and-values/) has exact house-by-house breakdowns. Gold bars are best but rare. Check kitchen counters and bedroom drawers first.

For Master thief (100 items), I recommend a Completionist mode run. Since there is no time pressure, you can methodically clear every container in each house. A single large house has 15-25 lootable items. Hit 5-6 houses and you are done.

For Golden card (3 heists), heist missions unlock around Rank 10 and appear on the map with a briefcase icon. Each heist takes 5-10 minutes with proper gear (blowtorch, drill, hacking tool). Plan your route so you can hit all 3 in one session.

Challenge Achievements

Achievement Requirement Notes
Delivery of a lifetime Complete all item request lists in one game Plan routes around requests
Full house Have 5 active jobs available at once Unlocks naturally by Rank 14
Cautious Win with max 3 police calls Hard mode recommended
Insane victory Complete the game on Master difficulty Hardest of the medium tier
Tree and free Escape police 3 times in one run After police calls, hide in alley until bar depletes

Delivery of a lifetime is the most time-sensitive. Item request lists appear at your hideout board. Each list has 3-5 items. You must deliver all of them in a single game (one session from start to finish). I recommend:

  1. Play on Standard difficulty (Completionist mode has more lists, making this harder)
  2. Do not sell any loot until you have checked the request board
  3. Stash requested items in your hideout storage
  4. Turn them in as soon as you have a full list

Cautious (max 3 police calls) requires careful play. Police are triggered by: alarms, being spotted by residents, breaking windows in view of neighbors, and using loud tools. Stick to silent methods: lockpicks over drills, silenced crowbar for doors, and always disable cameras first.

Progression Tips

  • Achievement hunting order: Tutorial > Rank > Loot > Challenges > Hard (see Part 2)
  • Most medium achievements can be combined in a single Completionist run
  • You can check your achievement progress in Steam Shift+Tab overlay without leaving the game
  • If you are stuck on a specific achievement, check the Steam Community guides linked below

Crime Simulator Achievement Guide Part 2: Hard Achievements

Jun 9, 2026

These are the achievements that took me real effort to unlock. Some require multiple playthroughs or very specific conditions. I would not attempt them until you have completed Standard mode and understand all the mechanics. If you have not started yet, the getting started guide covers the basics.

Challenge Achievements (15)

Challenges are optional objectives that appear during runs. Completing them in specific quantities unlocks these achievements.

Achievement Requirement Difficulty Strategy
Insane victory Complete 5 challenges in one game Medium Pick a medium house with multiple challenge types
Master challenger Complete 10 challenges in one game Hard Requires a large multi-room house
Challenge addict Complete 25 challenges total Medium Grind across multiple runs
Speed demon Complete 3 time-based challenges Hard Small apartment, clear rooms fast
Silent but deadly Complete 3 stealth challenges Medium Use silenced tools only
Heavy hitter Complete 3 destruction challenges Medium Break doors, windows, containers
All-rounder Complete one of each challenge type Very Hard Plan route for stealth + destruction + speed

For "All-rounder," I used a large house with multiple approaches. Ground floor for stealth, basement for destruction, upstairs for time-based tasks. One house can satisfy all challenge types if you plan your route. The house tier guide helps you pick a target with the right room variety.

Challenge achievements are easiest to stack. Pick a large suburban house and plan three challenge types in advance. Enter silently for stealth, smash containers for destruction, and sprint between rooms for speed. I got "Insane victory" and "Silent but deadly" in the same run.

Speed Achievements (5)

These have strict time requirements. I found "Speed runner" the easiest -- choose a small apartment, grab 2 items, exit. Took me about 90 seconds. A full tool upgrade speeds this up considerably -- faster lockpicks, quieter entry.

Achievement Requirement Strategy
Speed runner Complete a heist under 2 minutes Small apartment, grab 2 visible items, leave immediately
Quick getaway Escape police within 30 seconds Trigger minor alert near a closet hiding spot
Speed looter Steal 5 items in under 1 minute Pre-scout layout, sprint past everything, grab only small items
Rush hour Complete 3 heists in under 30 minutes Chain 3 small houses back to back
Delivery of a lifetime Complete item request within 1 minute Keep requested item in inventory, accept request, deliver fast

Speed runner strategy:

  1. Choose the smallest apartment on the job board
  2. Enter through an unlocked window (no lockpicking time)
  3. Grab the first 2 visible items on the kitchen counter
  4. Exit the same way you entered
  5. Total time: approximately 90 seconds

Rare Condition Achievements (10)

These require unusual circumstances that you would not encounter in normal play.

Achievement Requirement Difficulty Strategy
Three and free Escape a 3-star police chase Hard Trigger police deliberately, use speed boost if available
Millionaire Earn $1,000,000 total lifetime Very Hard Grind, expect 40-60 hours
Untouchable Complete a full game without arrests Very Hard Perfect play, no mistakes
Completionist Complete all locations in Completionist mode Hard Methodical, district-by-district clearing

Three and free -- I triggered it by deliberately smashing a window in front of a patrol car, then hiding in a closet until the timer dropped. The key is having good hiding spots memorized and not sprinting during the chase.

Millionaire is the grindiest achievement. At the rate I was earning in late game (roughly $2,000-$5,000 per hour), expect 40-60 hours of play. Completionist mode is much faster than quota-constrained runs because you can focus on high-value heists without time pressure.

Millionaire is easier in Completionist mode because you can take as many runs as needed without restarting. In Hardcore, every reset wipes your progress. If you are going for 100% achievements, plan a dedicated Completionist run for Millionaire and leave Hardcore for the score multiplier achievement.

Update 1.3 Achievements (10)

Achievement Requirement Difficulty Notes
Gold fever Smelt 10 gold bars Medium Requires gold bar collection across multiple runs
Jeweler Appraise 10 jewelry pieces Medium Must distinguish authentic from fake
Dig deep Fully upgrade the basement Hard Requires 18+ gold bars across all upgrades
PvP victor Win 3 PvP competitions Hard Requires good tools and combat skill
Master key Unlock the Master Key Hard Part of 1.3 progression chain

Gold fever requires collecting 10 gold bars. Gold bars spawn in high-security houses and heist locations. I found that targeted runs to houses with confirmed safes yielded the most bars. Average: 1 bar per 2-3 high-security houses.

Dig deep (18+ gold bars for full basement) is the hardest 1.3 achievement. The upgrade path: Storage Rack (2 bars), Tool Wall (3 bars), Security Door (5 bars), Workshop Bench (8 bars). Total: 18 bars. At 1 bar per 2-3 houses, expect 36-54 high-security house runs.

What I Found Most Useful

  • Stack achievements in single runs -- one large house can satisfy 3-4 requirements
  • Back up saves before attempting risky runs
  • The 100% Achievement Guide on Steam (linked below) is a work in progress but has the current community strategies
  • Use Completionist mode for "Millionaire" and most loot-based achievements
  • Save Hardcore mode for last -- you only need it for the 2x score bonus achievement

Achievement Progression Order

  1. Start with Speed achievements (fast, easy, teach map routes)
  2. Move to Challenge achievements (stack multiple per run)
  3. Grind Rare condition achievements (Millionaire takes the longest)
  4. Finish with Update 1.3 achievements (require endgame gear)
  5. Attempt Hardcore last (single mistake = full reset)
